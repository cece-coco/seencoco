18 U.S.C. §2257 Statement

seencoco.com — last updated 25 July 2026

Nature of the content

All imagery published on this site is digitally created artwork. It is not photography of any real human being. No actual person is depicted, and no real person was photographed, filmed, or recorded in its production.

Records-keeping

Because the content contains no depictions of actual human beings, the record-keeping requirements of 18 U.S.C. §2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75 do not apply to it. This statement is published for clarity rather than because such records are required.

Age representation

Every subject depicted is represented as an adult aged 18 years or older. Content depicting, or appearing to depict, minors is not produced, published, hosted, or sold here under any circumstance, and never will be.

Consent

All content is created and published by the sole operator of this site, of and as herself. No third party appears in any set.

Access

Access is restricted to persons 18 years of age or older, and of legal age in their own jurisdiction.
