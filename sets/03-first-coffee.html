SET 03 · 59 FRAMES

First Coffee

my kitchen, early

An oversized white shirt and nothing else is not an outfit. It is a decision you make at six in the morning and then stand by.

I am not a morning person and this is the closest I come. The machine takes four minutes and I stand there for all four of them, barefoot on cold tile, wearing something I did not choose so much as pick up off the floor.

The shirt is his. Obviously the shirt is his. That is the entire appeal of the shirt and pretending otherwise would be insulting to both of us.

The light in this one is the best light in any of the seven — that flat early sun through a kitchen window that makes everything look like it is happening inside somebody's memory.

And then the coffee went cold, because I did not drink it.
