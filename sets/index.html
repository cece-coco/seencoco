The sets

Seven of them. Fifty-nine frames each, shot one room at a time.

Every set has a free half and a paid half, and the split is not a tease — the free frames are full resolution and nothing has been done to them. They are simply the part of the evening where I was still dressed.

The paid half is on a different machine. What is on this website is a wrecked copy — scaled down to sixty-four pixels and blurred, so there is genuinely nothing left in the file to recover. I would rather show you honestly small than pretend a filter is a lock.
