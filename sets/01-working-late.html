SET 01 · 59 FRAMES

Working Late

my own office, gone eleven

The set is called Working Late because that is exactly what it was. I did not go upstairs.

There is a particular hour — somewhere past eleven, when the build is running and there is nothing to do but wait for it — where I stop being a person who is working and start being a person sitting alone in a warm room in expensive underwear.

I did not plan the green. I own it because somebody bought it for me and I had never once had a reason to put it on, and then one night I was going to be at this desk until two anyway and I thought: well, nobody is coming up here.

The first frames are the honest ones. That is genuinely what I look like at that desk with the monitors going — the light is teal because the screens are teal, and I am half-turned because something on the left one had just finished.

What happens after that is what happens when the build finishes and I do not get up.

the free half

Four of the first twelve frames. Full resolution, nothing removed — these are simply the part of the set where I still had clothes on.

the rest of it

Those four are wrecked on purpose. Not blurred over — destroyed: scaled down to sixty-four pixels, blurred, and scaled back up, so the detail is not hidden somewhere in the file, it was never written to it. There is nothing there to recover. The real ones are on a machine you cannot reach from this page.

Fifty-nine frames. It does not stay at the desk.
