The Reading Room

the good chair, the tall lamp

I have never once finished a book in that chair, and this set is a fairly complete explanation of why.

It is a deep chair and a warm lamp and a room with a door that closes, and the honest truth is that I go in there to read for about eleven minutes and then I am simply somewhere else entirely, still holding the book.

Something about being comfortable undoes me far faster than anything designed to. Lingerie is a costume. A chair you have sunk into with your knees up is not, and that is exactly why it goes further.

The early frames are what it actually looks like. Nothing arranged. That is the position I am genuinely in at nine at night when nobody has asked me anything for an hour.

the free half

Four of the first twelve frames. Full resolution, nothing removed — these are simply the part of the set where I still had clothes on.

the rest of it

Those four are wrecked on purpose. Not blurred over — destroyed: scaled down to sixty-four pixels, blurred, and scaled back up, so the detail is not hidden somewhere in the file, it was never written to it.
