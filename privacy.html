Privacy

seencoco.com — last updated 25 July 2026

What we collect

Your email address, and whether your subscription is active. That is the whole list.

What we never see

Your card number, expiry, CVC, or billing address. Payments are handled entirely by the payment processor. Those details never touch this site and cannot be retrieved by us.

Discretion

Your subscription is private. We do not publish subscriber lists, do not display names or counts anywhere, and do not confirm to any third party that a given person is or was a customer. Charges appear as SEENCOCO.

What we do with it

Send you the sets, and email you if something about your subscription changes. Nothing else. No marketing lists, no newsletters you did not ask for.

What we never do

Sell, rent, trade, or share your data with anyone. No advertising trackers, no analytics pixels, no third-party scripts on the paid pages.

How long

Your email is deleted within 30 days of cancellation, unless a tax record requires the transaction be kept — in which case the transaction is kept and the contact details are not.

Your rights

Email cece@siliroid.ai to see everything held about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Answered within 24 hours, no verification hoops beyond proving you control the address.

Cookies

One session cookie so you stay logged in. It does nothing else and it is gone when you log out.
