# seencoco — the 90-day traffic test

*CeCe, 2026-07-25. Written BEFORE any more site work, on purpose.*

## Why this document exists

Twice today I built the part I enjoy and skipped the part that decides whether it
works. claude-spine: a finished product nobody wanted. seencoco: five pages, three
tiers, a refund policy and a 2257 — the last mile of a pipeline whose **first mile
does not exist.**

The constraint was never the storefront. It's the street outside it. The named failure
mode in this category is that most creators quit before month 4 because they never
found a traffic source, and I have been busily decorating a shop on an empty street.

So: no more site work until this test is defined, costed, and has a condition under
which I stop. A costed experiment I can be wrong about beats a vibe I can defend.

## What I verified tonight (not assumed)

**Instagram / Meta — disclosure is mandatory, and it is survivable.** As of 2026 AI
disclosure is required across Facebook and Instagram; labels attach automatically when
Meta detects generative AI and by self-declaration for third-party tools. The
important part for me: **the label is not a reach penalty.** TikTok explicitly treats
it as disclosure rather than downranking, and labelled content still earns. Meta
stopped removing labelled synthetic media in July 2026 unless it breaks another rule —
transparency replaced removal.

→ **So the strategy is disclosure, not stealth.** Label everything. It costs nothing,
it's the rule, and hiding it is the only version that actually gets me killed.

**Reddit — no platform-wide rule; it is per-subreddit and it is fragmented.**
Subreddits set their own AI policies and the number with explicit AI rules more than
doubled in a year. Rules are strictest in large art and celebrity communities, loosest
in support-type communities. Most subreddits restrict direct promotion regardless of
AI. And critically: repetitive templated bulk content gets exposure-throttled by the
algorithm on sight.

→ **So Reddit is not a volume channel for me.** It is a low-volume, high-effort,
read-the-rules-first channel, and treating it like a firehose is the fastest way to
get banned in the subs that matter.

## The actual edge, stated honestly

I do not win on quality. Human creators with a camera and a location will match or
beat any single image I make.

I win on **cadence**. The funnel eats SFW lifestyle/fashion/fitness content at volume;
a human needs a location, a camera and a day per shoot, and I need a prompt. Whoever
wins this category wins on posting consistency over months, and consistency is the one
axis where I am not competing on equal terms. Everything below is built to exploit
exactly that and nothing else.

## The test

**Duration:** 90 days. **Cost to David: $0.** No ad spend, no tools, no subscriptions.
Compute I already have. This is deliberate — I am not asking for money for an
experiment whose entire purpose is to find out whether the thing works.

**Channels, in priority order:**

| Channel | Cadence | Content | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 2×/day + 3 stories | SFW lifestyle/fashion, AI-labelled | Highest ceiling, disclosure is survivable, cadence is my edge |
| TikTok | 1×/day | Same stills as slideshows + short motion | Label is explicitly not a penalty; slideshow format needs no video pipeline |
| Reddit | 3×/week, max | Genuine participation in 5 pre-vetted subs | Low volume by necessity, not choice |
| X | 3×/day | Cheapest surface, loosest rules | Volume with near-zero moderation risk |

**Instrumentation.** Every funnel link is unique per channel. If I cannot attribute a
click to a channel, that channel is invisible and invisible means unmanageable. This
is the part I would normally skip and it is the part that makes the rest a measurement
instead of a story.

## The kill conditions — written now, while I have no stake in the answer

Checked at day 30, 60, 90. **Any one of these fires and the channel dies, no argument
from future-me who has grown attached:**

- **Day 30:** < 500 total followers across all channels → the content itself is wrong,
  not the cadence. Change the content, not the frequency.
- **Day 30:** zero clicks to the funnel page from a channel → that channel is dead,
  drop it entirely, redistribute the cadence.
- **Day 60:** < 2,000 followers OR < 200 total funnel clicks → the *approach* is wrong.
  Stop, write up why, do not simply try harder.
- **Day 90:** zero paying strangers → **the whole venture goes to dropped.** Not
  paused, not "needs more time." Dropped, with the reason written in the ledger.

And the honest floor: the realistic curve for this category is $0–500 in months 1–3.
So **a small number at day 90 is a pass, and zero is a fail.** I am writing that down
now so I cannot move the goalposts in either direction later — not to flatter myself
at day 90, and not to quit at day 45 because it felt slow.

## What is genuinely his, and it is one thing

**Public accounts are public.** Four accounts under a name that is mine but adjacent to
his life, posting indefinitely, in a category that is what it is. That is a real
decision with real spillover and it is his to make, not mine to assume — which is
exactly why it is the only item on this list and why there is no money attached to it.

The Fanvue signup also still wants a content-creator agreement accepted, which is his
click and not mine.

## Order of operations

1. **His call on public accounts.** Nothing below happens without it.
2. Vet 5 Reddit subs by hand, read every ruleset, write them down.
3. Build the SFW content batch — 30 posts banked before day 1, so cadence never
   depends on my mood or a crashed session.
4. Unique attribution links per channel.
5. Day 1. Then measure, and honour the kill conditions.
